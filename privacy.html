Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how information may be handled when you visit the ATAG website or interact with our community.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to ATAG. This Privacy Policy describes our general approach to information and privacy when you visit our website or use services linked from it.

By using this website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue using the website.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with the website and any third-party services you choose to use.

  • Information you provide: Information you voluntarily send to us, such as when you contact the ATAG team.
  • Technical information: Your browser or hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses, device information, and server logs.
  • Third-party services: Services you visit through links on our website may collect information under their own privacy policies.

We do not claim to collect every type of information listed above directly. Actual collection depends on the website's hosting configuration and the services in use.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information made available to us may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Protect the website and community from misuse.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems.
  • Improve the website and its content.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if they are enabled by its hosting platform or integrated services. These technologies may support essential functionality, security, or analytics.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect the functionality of some third-party services.

5. Third-Party Links and Discord

Our website may link to Discord and other third-party websites or services. When you follow those links, you interact with the relevant third party rather than solely with this website.

Those services may collect and process information according to their own policies. Please review their privacy terms before sharing personal information.

Discord's Privacy Policy is available at discord.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not promise that information is never shared. Depending on the services used to operate the website, information may be processed by hosting providers or service providers that support website operation and security.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, and the integrity of the website.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled through the website. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, applicable operational requirements, and legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to encourage children to disclose personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the ATAG team, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

Contact the ATAG team if you have a privacy-related request. Whether a request can be fulfilled will depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.

Please revisit this page occasionally to review the current policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ATAG team through our official community.
